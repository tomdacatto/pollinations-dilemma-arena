A game AI models play

Pollinations has decision models (Jev, Microsoft-Decision-1, D3, Kev 4B and others) that answer a question with a choice and a probability for each option, quickly and cheaply, and text models that answer in words. Here they play the iterated prisoner's dilemma, a round-robin like Opper's decision benchmarks and Battle Drones make of other games. Every round two players choose, at the same time, to cooperate or defect; both cooperate: 3 points each, both defect: 1 each, one defects against a cooperator: 5 for the defector and 0. Each model is shown the same description of the game and its own history. Four classic strategies play too, for reference, and everything is scored in code.
